CHONGQING & CHENGDU

Discover the stunning contrasts of Southwest China — from Chongqing's dramatic mountain landscapes and futuristic architecture to Chengdu's ancient traditions and famous pandas. This guide helps you plan a focused 5-day journey through both cities.

VIEW ITINERARY WATCH VIDEOS

CITY HISTORY & CULTURE

Explore the rich heritage of Chongqing and Chengdu

Chongqing has a history spanning over 3,000 years, serving as the capital of the Ba State during the Warring States period. During World War II, it served as the provisional capital of China from 1937 to 1946, which led to significant industrial development and population growth.


The city is famous for its spicy Sichuan cuisine, particularly Chongqing hot pot, featuring numbing Sichuan peppercorns and fiery chili peppers. Food culture is integral to daily life, with hot pot restaurants found throughout the city.


Chengdu, the capital of Sichuan province, boasts a history dating back over 4,000 years. It is renowned as the home of giant pandas, Sichuan opera, and tea house culture.

TRAVEL ESSENTIALS

Important information for your journey

Transportation

High-speed trains connect Chongqing and Chengdu in ~1.5 hours. Book in advance via Trip.com or the China Railway app.

Food & Dining

Try Chongqing hot pot and Chengdu street food. For mild spice, request “微辣” (wei la).

Winter Travel

Dress in layers. Indoor attractions are heated. Pandas are typically more active in cooler weather.

5-DAY TRAVEL ITINERARY

Chongqing & Chengdu Travel Plan 重慶成都計劃

Day 1: Chongqing Arrival & Urban Introduction 第一天：到達重慶與城市介紹

14:00-15:30

Arrival & Hotel Check-in 到達與酒店入住

Arrive in Chongqing and check into your hotel. Recommended areas: Jiefangbei (解放碑) for convenience or near Hongyadong (洪崖洞) for scenic views. Winter Tip: Ensure your hotel has good heating as Chongqing can be damp and chilly in winter.

15:30-17:30

Jiefangbei Exploration 解放碑探索

Explore Jiefangbei Pedestrian Street (解放碑步行街), the commercial heart of Chongqing. Visit the People's Liberation Monument (重慶人民解放紀念碑). Winter: Dress in warm layers; malls nearby are good warm breaks.

17:30-19:00

Hongyadong & Qiansimen Bridge 洪崖洞與千廝門大橋

Visit Hongyadong (洪崖洞), the iconic stilted complex built into the hillside. After dark, walk across Qiansimen Bridge (千廝門大橋) for skyline views. Winter: The famous fog can create dramatic photos.

19:00-20:30

Chongqing Hot Pot Experience 重慶火鍋體驗

Enjoy your first authentic Chongqing hot pot (重慶火鍋). Traditional is very spicy! Winter: Perfect for cold evenings.

For sensitive spice levels: “微辣 wēi là” (mild) or “不辣 bù là” (not spicy). Sichuan peppercorns (花椒 huājiāo) create a numbing sensation.

Day 2: Chongqing Urban Exploration 第二天：重慶城市探索

09:00-11:30

Ciqikou Ancient Town 磁器口古鎮

Visit Ciqikou (磁器口古鎮) along the Jialing River (嘉陵江). Wander narrow streets, browse crafts, and sample snacks. Winter: Early mornings are calmer; many shops offer warm indoor seating.

11:30-13:00

Lunch & Travel to Liziba 午餐與前往李子壩

Lunch nearby, then take transit to Liziba Station (李子壩站).

13:00-14:30

Liziba Monorail Experience 李子壩單軌體驗

See Line 2 (輕軌2號線) pass through a residential building. Use the viewing platform for photos. Winter: The monorail is sheltered between stops.

14:30-16:30

E'ling Park & City Views 鵝嶺公園與城市景觀

Visit E'ling Park (鵝嶺公園) for classic panoramas where the Jialing (嘉陵江) meets the Yangtze (長江). Winter: Exposed viewpoints — bring a warm outer layer; clear days can be excellent.

16:30-18:00

Guotai Art Center & Linjiangmen 國泰藝術中心與臨江門

Stop by Guotai Art Center (國泰藝術中心) for its facade, then explore food stalls at Linjiangmen (臨江門).

18:00-20:00

Dinner & Evening 晚餐與夜晚活動

Try another hot pot style, grilled fish (烤魚 kǎo yú), or local noodles (小麵 xiǎo miàn).

Day 3: Chongqing History & Transfer to Chengdu 第三天：重慶歷史與轉移到成都

09:00-11:30

Historical Museums 歷史博物館

Visit the Chongqing Flying Tigers Museum (重慶飛虎隊博物館) and Stilwell Museum (史迪威博物館) to learn about WWII-era Chongqing. Winter: Comfortable indoor culture.

11:30-13:00

Alternative: Sichuan Fine Arts Institute 替代方案：四川美術學院

Visit the Sichuan Fine Arts Institute (四川美術學院) area for murals and street art.

13:00-14:00

Final Chongqing Lunch 最後的重慶午餐

Last meal ideas: Dan Dan Noodles (擔擔麵) or Chongqing Xiao Mian (重慶小麵).

14:00-15:30

Travel to Railway Station 前往火車站

Check out and travel to Chongqing West (重慶西站) or Shapingba (沙坪壩站) for the high-speed train.

16:00-17:30

High-Speed Train to Chengdu 高鐵前往成都

Take the high-speed train (~1.5 hours). Winter: Climate-controlled and comfortable.

Book in advance via Trip.com or China Railway app. 提前通過Trip.com或中國鐵路應用程序預訂火車票
17:30-19:00

Chengdu Hotel Check-in 成都酒店入住

Arrive at Chengdu East (成都東站), transfer to your hotel. Areas: near Jinli/Wuhou Temple (錦里/武侯祠) or Chunxi Road (春熙路).

19:00-20:30

First Chengdu Dinner 首次成都晚餐

Try Chuan Chuan Xiang (串串香) or Sichuan classics for your first night.

Day 4: Chengdu Pandas & Ancient Culture 第四天：成都熊貓與古代文化

07:30-08:30

Early Start for Panda Base 早起前往熊貓基地

Early breakfast, then head to Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding (成都大熊貓繁育研究基地). Timing: Most active 8:30–10:00.

08:30-12:00

Chengdu Panda Base Visit 成都熊貓基地參觀

Spend 3–4 hours with giant pandas (大熊貓) and red pandas (小熊貓). Winter is often great for activity levels.

Buy tickets online to avoid queues; wear comfortable shoes — lots of walking. 提前在線購票避免排隊
12:00-13:30

Lunch Near Panda Base 熊貓基地附近午餐

Lunch nearby, then head back toward the city center.

13:30-15:30

Wuhou Shrine Museum 武侯祠博物館

Visit Wuhou Shrine (武侯祠), dedicated to Zhuge Liang (諸葛亮) and Three Kingdoms (三國) history.

15:30-18:30

Jinli Ancient Street 錦里古街

Explore Jinli (錦里古街) for snacks and shopping. Many tea houses provide indoor seating in winter.

18:30-20:00

Dinner at Jinli or Nearby 錦里或附近晚餐

Dinner ideas: Zhong Dumplings (鐘水餃), Dan Dan Noodles (擔擔麵), or Chengdu barbecue (成都燒烤).

Day 5: Chengdu Culture & Departure 第五天：成都文化與離開

09:00-11:00

Chengdu Museum 成都博物館

Visit Chengdu Museum (成都博物館) (free entry, may require booking) or Sichuan Museum (四川博物院).

11:00-13:00

People's Park & Heming Tea House 人民公園與鶴鳴茶社

Stroll People's Park (人民公園) and visit Heming Tea House (鶴鳴茶社) for tea, mahjong, and local vibes.

13:00-14:30

Lunch in People's Park Area 人民公園區域午餐

Lunch nearby, then continue exploring.

14:30-16:30

Kuanzhai Alley Exploration 寬窄巷子探索

Visit Kuanzhai Alley (寬窄巷子) for restored Qing-era lanes, shops, and courtyards.

16:30-18:00

Final Shopping & Packing 最後購物與打包

Last-minute souvenir shopping, then return to pack.

18:00-20:00

Farewell Sichuan Opera Dinner 告別川劇晚餐

Enjoy a farewell Sichuan Opera (川劇) performance. Shufeng Yayun Teahouse (蜀風雅韻茶館) is a popular choice.

Book tickets in advance; dinner-show and performance-only options are both common. 提前預訂歌劇票
20:00+

Airport Transfer or Extended Stay 機場轉移或延長停留

Transfer to Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport (成都雙流國際機場), or extend your stay in Sichuan.
