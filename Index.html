CHONGQING & CHENGDU

Discover the stunning contrasts of Southwest China - from Chongqing's dramatic mountain landscapes and futuristic architecture to Chengdu's ancient traditions and famous pandas. This comprehensive guide will help you plan the perfect 5-day journey through these incredible cities.

CITY HISTORY & CULTURE

Explore the rich heritage of Chongqing and Chengdu

Chongqing has a history spanning over 3,000 years, serving as the capital of the Ba State during the Warring States period. During World War II, it served as the provisional capital of China from 1937 to 1946, which led to significant industrial development and population growth.

The city is famous for its spicy Sichuan cuisine, particularly Chongqing hot pot, which features numbing Sichuan peppercorns and fiery chili peppers. The local food culture is an integral part of daily life, with hot pot restaurants found throughout the city.

Chengdu, the capital of Sichuan province, boasts a history dating back over 4,000 years. It has served as a capital for various kingdoms and is renowned as the home of giant pandas, Sichuan opera, and exquisite tea house culture.

TRAVEL ESSENTIALS

Important information for your journey

Transportation

High-speed trains connect Chongqing and Chengdu in 1.5 hours. Book tickets in advance via Trip.com or China Railway app.

Food & Dining

Try Chongqing hot pot and Chengdu's famous street food. For mild spice, request "微辣" (wei la).

Winter Travel

Dress in layers. Indoor attractions are heated. Pandas are more active in cooler weather.

Day 1: Chongqing Arrival & Urban Introduction 第一天：到達重慶與城市介紹

14:00-15:30

Arrival & Hotel Check-in 到達與酒店入住

Arrive in Chongqing and check into your hotel. Recommended areas: Jiefangbei (解放碑) for convenience or near Hongyadong (洪崖洞) for scenic views. Winter Tip: Ensure your hotel has good heating as Chongqing can be damp and chilly in winter.

15:30-17:30

Jiefangbei Exploration 解放碑探索

Explore Jiefangbei Pedestrian Street (解放碑步行街), the bustling commercial heart of Chongqing. Visit the Chongqing People's Liberation Monument (重慶人民解放紀念碑). Winter Consideration: This area is mostly outdoors, so dress in warm layers. The shopping malls provide warm refuge if needed.

17:30-19:00

Hongyadong & Qiansimen Bridge 洪崖洞與千廝門大橋

Visit Hongyadong (洪崖洞), the incredible stilted building complex built into the hillside. As darkness falls, walk across Qiansimen Bridge (千廝門大橋) for spectacular night views of the city skyline. Winter Advantage: The famous Chongqing fog creates dramatic atmospheric photos in winter.

19:00-20:30

Chongqing Hot Pot Experience 重慶火鍋體驗

Enjoy your first authentic Chongqing Hot Pot (重慶火鍋) dinner. Choose your spice level - traditional is very spicy! Winter Benefit: Hot pot is perfect for cold weather, warming you from the inside out.

For those sensitive to spice, request "微辣 wēi là" (mild spicy) or "不辣 bù là" (not spicy). The broth with Sichuan peppercorns (花椒 huājiāo) creates a numbing sensation.

Day 2: Chongqing Urban Exploration 第二天：重慶城市探索

09:00-11:30

Ciqikou Ancient Town 磁器口古鎮

Visit Ciqikou Ancient Town (磁器口古鎮), perched on the hillside along the Jialing River (嘉陵江). Wander the narrow streets, browse traditional crafts, and sample local snacks. Winter Planning: Mornings are less crowded. Many shops provide indoor seating with heating.

11:30-13:00

Lunch & Travel to Liziba 午餐與前往李子壩

Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant near Ciqikou. Then take public transportation to Liziba Station (李子壩站).

13:00-14:30

Liziba Monorail Experience 李子壩單軌體驗

Witness the famous Light Rail Line 2 (輕軌2號線) passing through a residential building at Liziba Station. Use the designated viewing platform for photos. Winter Advantage: The monorail provides sheltered transportation between locations.

14:30-16:30

E'ling Park & City Views 鵝嶺公園與城市景觀

Visit E'ling Park (鵝嶺公園) for the classic panoramic view where the Jialing River (嘉陵江) meets the Yangtze River (長江). The viewing platform offers the iconic Chongqing photo opportunity. Winter Consideration: The park is exposed, so wear warm clothing. On clear winter days, visibility can be excellent.

16:30-18:00

Guotai Art Center & Linjiangmen 國泰藝術中心與臨江門

Visit the Guotai Art Center (國泰藝術中心) to see its unique sparkling facade, then explore the food stalls at Linjiangmen (臨江門).

18:00-20:00

Dinner & Evening 晚餐與夜晚活動

Try a different style of hot pot or sample other Chongqing specialties like grilled fish (烤魚 kǎo yú) or local noodles (小麵 xiǎo miàn).

Day 3: Chongqing History & Transfer to Chengdu 第三天：重慶歷史與轉移到成都

09:00-11:30

Historical Museums 歷史博物館

Visit the Chongqing Flying Tigers Museum (重慶飛虎隊博物館) and Stilwell Museum (史迪威博物館) to learn about Chongqing's crucial role in WWII as China's provisional capital and the cooperation between Chinese and American forces. Winter Advantage: These indoor activities provide a warm cultural experience.

11:30-13:00

Alternative: Sichuan Fine Arts Institute 替代方案：四川美術學院

For a different experience, visit the Sichuan Fine Arts Institute (四川美術學院) to see the incredible graffiti and street art that covers the area.

13:00-14:00

Final Chongqing Lunch 最後的重慶午餐

Enjoy a last Chongqing meal - perhaps Dan Dan Noodles (擔擔麵 dàn dàn miàn) or Chongqing Xiao Mian (重慶小麵).

14:00-15:30

Travel to Railway Station 前往火車站

Check out of your hotel and travel to Chongqing West Station (重慶西站) or Shapingba Station (沙坪壩站) for your high-speed train to Chengdu.

16:00-17:30

High-Speed Train to Chengdu 高鐵前往成都

Take the high-speed train from Chongqing to Chengdu (approximately 1.5 hours). Winter Tip: Trains are climate-controlled and comfortable regardless of outside weather.

Book train tickets in advance via Trip.com or the China Railway app, especially during holiday periods. 提前通過Trip.com或中國鐵路應用程序預訂火車票
17:30-19:00

Chengdu Hotel Check-in 成都酒店入住

Arrive at Chengdu East Station (成都東站), transfer to your hotel, and check in. Recommended areas: near Jinli/Wuhou Temple (錦里/武侯祠) for cultural access or Chunxi Road (春熙路) for shopping convenience.

19:00-20:30

First Chengdu Dinner 首次成都晚餐

Experience Chengdu's famous food culture with Chuan Chuan Xiang (串串香) or visit a local restaurant for Sichuan classics. Winter Benefit: Chengdu's spicy cuisine is perfect for cold weather.

Day 4: Chengdu Pandas & Ancient Culture 第四天：成都熊貓與古代文化

07:30-08:30

Early Start for Panda Base 早起前往熊貓基地

Have an early breakfast and travel to the Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding (成都大熊貓繁育研究基地). Critical Timing: Pandas are most active in the morning, especially during feeding time (8:30-10:00).

08:30-12:00

Chengdu Panda Base Visit 成都熊貓基地參觀

Spend 3-4 hours observing giant pandas (大熊貓) and red pandas (小熊貓). Visit the nursery if there are cubs. The pandas are more active in cooler weather, making winter an excellent time for observation. Winter Advantage: Pandas tend to be more active in cooler temperatures than in summer heat.

Purchase tickets online in advance to avoid queues. The base is large with considerable walking, so wear comfortable shoes. 提前在線購票避免排隊
12:00-13:30

Lunch Near Panda Base 熊貓基地附近午餐

Have lunch at one of the restaurants near the Panda Base before heading back to the city center.

13:30-15:30

Wuhou Shrine Museum 武侯祠博物館

Visit Wuhou Shrine (武侯祠), a temple complex dedicated to Zhuge Liang (諸葛亮) and heroes of the Three Kingdoms period (三國時期). The indoor exhibits provide a warm cultural experience.

15:30-18:30

Jinli Ancient Street 錦里古街

Explore Jinli Ancient Street (錦里古街) adjacent to Wuhou Shrine. This bustling pedestrian street is perfect for trying multiple Chengdu snacks in one place and shopping for souvenirs. Winter Planning: Many food stalls and tea houses provide indoor seating with heating.

18:30-20:00

Dinner at Jinli or Nearby 錦里或附近晚餐

Enjoy dinner at Jinli Ancient Street or a nearby restaurant. Sample local specialties like Zhong Dumplings (鐘水餃), Dan Dan Noodles (擔擔麵), or Chengdu-style barbecue (成都燒烤).

Day 5: Chengdu Culture & Departure 第五天：成都文化與離開

09:00-11:00

Chengdu Museum 成都博物館

Visit the Chengdu Museum (成都博物館) (free entry, requires advance booking) or Sichuan Museum (四川博物院) to deepen your understanding of the region's history and culture. Winter Advantage: Indoor museum visit provides a comfortable cultural experience regardless of weather.

11:00-13:00

People's Park & Heming Tea House 人民公園與鶴鳴茶社

Stroll through People's Park (人民公園) to experience local Chengdu life. Visit the historic Heming Tea House (鶴鳴茶社) to drink tea, play mahjong (麻將), and potentially experience traditional ear cleaning (掏耳朵). Winter Consideration: The tea house is partially open-air but provides heaters and warm spaces.

13:00-14:30

Lunch in People's Park Area 人民公園區域午餐

Enjoy lunch at one of the restaurants near People's Park before your final Chengdu explorations.

14:30-16:30

Kuanzhai Alley Exploration 寬窄巷子探索

Visit Kuanzhai Alley (寬窄巷子), a restored Qing-dynasty area with wide, narrow, and well alleys. Explore the shops, cafes, and courtyards.

16:30-18:00

Final Shopping & Packing 最後購物與打包

Last-minute souvenir shopping and return to your hotel to pack for departure.

18:00-20:00

Farewell Sichuan Opera Dinner 告別川劇晚餐

Enjoy a farewell dinner with a Sichuan Opera (川劇) performance. The Shufeng Yayun Teahouse (蜀風雅韻茶館) in Culture Park offers classic shows featuring face-changing (變臉), fire-spitting (噴火), and puppet acts (木偶戲). Winter Advantage: Indoor cultural performance provides an entertaining evening regardless of weather.

Book opera tickets in advance. You can choose dinner shows or performance-only options. 提前預訂歌劇票
20:00+

Airport Transfer or Extended Stay 機場轉移或延長停留

Transfer to Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport (成都雙流國際機場) for your departure flight, or extend your stay to explore more of Sichuan province.
