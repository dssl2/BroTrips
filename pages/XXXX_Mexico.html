7-Day Mexican Fiesta

The perfect introduction to Mexico's diverse experiences

Day 1: Arrival
Day 2: Beach Day
Day 3: Culture
Day 4: Adventure
Day 5: Relaxation
Day 6: Exploration

Day 1: Arrival in Paradise

Afternoon

Arrival in Cancún

Arrive at Cancún International Airport (CUN) and transfer to your luxury resort. Check in and take time to relax after your flight.

Consider booking a spa treatment to unwind after your journey
Evening

Welcome Dinner

Enjoy a gourmet Mexican welcome dinner at your resort's finest restaurant, featuring local specialties and live mariachi music.

Day 2: Beach & Relaxation

Morning

Beachfront Breakfast

Enjoy a delicious breakfast with ocean views at your resort's beachfront restaurant.

Midday

Beach Day

Spend the day relaxing on the pristine white sand beaches of the Riviera Maya. Enjoy swimming in the turquoise waters or try water sports like paddleboarding or kayaking.

Evening

Sunset Cocktails

Sip margaritas while watching the spectacular Caribbean sunset from your resort's beach bar.

Day 3: Cultural Experience

Morning

Tulum Ruins Tour

Visit the spectacular Mayan ruins of Tulum, perched on cliffs overlooking the Caribbean Sea. Learn about Mayan history and culture from your expert guide.

Afternoon

Cenote Swim & Lunch

Cool off with a swim in a sacred cenote (natural sinkhole), then enjoy a traditional Yucatecan lunch at a local restaurant.

Day 4: Adventure Day

Morning

Xcaret Park

Spend the day at Xcaret eco-archaeological park, where you can snorkel in underground rivers, see wildlife, and learn about Mexican culture.

Evening

Xcaret México Espectacular

Experience this spectacular night show that takes you on a journey through Mexico's history with music, dance, and colorful costumes.

Day 5: Resort Relaxation

Morning

Spa Day

Indulge in a luxurious spa treatment at your resort, perhaps trying a traditional temazcal (Mayan sweat lodge) experience.

Afternoon

Poolside Relaxation

Lounge by the resort's infinity pool with a good book and refreshing cocktails served to your cabana.

Day 6: Island Exploration

Morning

Isla Mujeres Catamaran Tour

Sail to the beautiful Isla Mujeres on a luxury catamaran. Enjoy snorkeling in the crystal-clear waters and time to explore the charming island.

Evening

Farewell Fiesta

Celebrate your last night in Mexico with a festive beach party featuring live music, folkloric dancers, and a gourmet buffet of Mexican specialties.

Day 7: Departure

Morning

Final Relaxation

Enjoy your final morning in Mexico with a leisurely breakfast and some last-minute beach time before your transfer to the airport.

Afternoon

Airport Transfer

Transfer to Cancún International Airport for your departure flight, taking with you unforgettable memories of Mexico.
