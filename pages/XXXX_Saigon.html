Day 1: Arrival & First Impressions

Ease into Saigon's rhythm and explore your surroundings

Afternoon

Arrival & Hotel Check-in

Settle into your accommodation in District 1, the heart of the city. Recommended areas: around Nguyen Hue Walking Street or Dong Khoi Street.

Try to book a hotel with a rooftop pool to enjoy Saigon's skyline from above
Evening

Sunset at Bitexco Sky Deck

Head to the 49th floor observation deck of Bitexco Financial Tower for panoramic views of the city as the sun sets.

Night

Dinner at Secret Garden

Enjoy authentic Vietnamese cuisine in this charming rooftop restaurant hidden down an alleyway.

Day 2: Historical Saigon

Discover the city's complex past and wartime history

Morning

War Remnants Museum

A sobering but essential visit to understand Vietnam's wartime experience from a local perspective.

Midday

Reunification Palace

Explore this perfectly preserved 1960s government building where the Vietnam War officially ended.

Afternoon

Notre-Dame Cathedral & Central Post Office

Admire these beautiful examples of French colonial architecture in the heart of the city.
